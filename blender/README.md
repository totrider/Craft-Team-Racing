# Blender models

Source files for models that are meshes rather than boxes. `blockbench/` holds the block-style models; this folder is its sibling.

    blender/<family>/<name>.blend

The `.blend` is the source. `python tools/export_blender_models.py` (run by `build-and-install`) opens every one in Blender in the background and writes, for each top-level
collection, `models/item/<collection>.obj` + `.mtl` and `models/item/pickup_<collection>.json` (NeoForge's OBJ loader) under `src/main/resources/assets/craftteamracing`.
Never edit those outputs by hand. Without Blender installed (set `BLENDER` to its path if it is not in Program Files) the committed outputs are used as they are.

Conventions (details in `tools/blender_export_models.py`):
- **One collection = one model**, named as the model. A collection whose name starts with `_` is not exported. The custom property `texture` on the collection names its picture in
  `textures/item/` (without `.png`); without it the collection's name is used.
- 1 Blender unit = 1 block. Blender's origin is the middle of the block's floor; +X east, +Y north, +Z up. (Minecraft x = X + 0.5, y = Z, z = 0.5 - Y.)
- UVs as Blender has them. Faces must point outward (Mesh > Normals > Recalculate Outside). The export triangulates and gives every face the same normal.
- Textures are generated by scripts in `tools/` (never paint the PNGs); the `.blend` only references them so you see them in the viewport.

Files:
- `shield/slime_shield.blend` - the Slime Shield (the Power Shield): `shield_green`, `shield_blue` (the slime's shell), `shield_dark` (its nucleus, taken from the screen) and
  `shield_sheen_0..7` (the glint's steps). Made once by `tools/make_slime_shield.py`; after that it is yours. The shapes follow the frame of the original's shield models
  (a squashed pyramid on the model's y axis, 1.94 x 1.94 x 0.71) so `client/ShieldBubble`'s poses and animation tables apply unchanged.
- `mask/mask_beam.blend` - the Totem's swirl: `mask_beam_aku_0..23` and `mask_beam_uka_0..23` (three tapering ribbons winding up the ring the head orbits on, the helix turned 10 degrees a frame, a seamless 24 frame loop). Made once by `tools/make_mask_beam.py`.
- `bomb/bomb.blend` - the Bomb: `bomb` and `bomb_charged` (the same mesh, the iron and the glowing texture): a sphere flattened at the two ends of its axle with a skull plate on each, a steel belt, a collar and a rope fuse ending where `BombFx` puts the flames. Made once by `tools/make_bomb_model.py`.
