# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - first release
- Kart with the original's physics: powerslide, hop, boost presses, start boost, spin-outs, wall slams, hang time, four engine classes as tuning profiles.
- Smooth driving over blocks, surfaces from block tags, Boost and Super Boost blocks, Fusion and copycat support.
- Track building (Start, Checkpoint and Podium nodes, Track Tool, test drive, branches) and races (gathering, grid, lamps, start rev, laps, positions, HUD, respawn, podium ceremony, redstone outputs).
- Viewing Console: watch a track's race from a block, at any time, with the racer's own screen.
- Fruit, crates and nine power-ups with charged forms; Rulebook, Drop Table, Track Music Sheet; trigger crates and the redstone linking node.
- Survival: Power Palm, Power Fruit chain, wood set, wheels, crates, recipes for everything; the kart as an item with paint; upgrades (Offroad Tires, Soft Bumper, Floater, Obsidian Floater, Paddle, Jump Spring, Low Gravity Module).
- Interaction with mobs, boats, minecarts and players on foot, and Minecraft's damage on karts, all configurable.
- Optional local resource packs from your own disc (sounds, music, textures) via the asset extractor; the mod itself ships no original assets.
