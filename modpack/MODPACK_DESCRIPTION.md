# <center>![Craft Team Racing title](TITLE_IMAGE_URL)</center>

**A kart racing modpack built around the Craft Team Racing mod: great performance, a big building toolbox and everything you need to design tracks and race on them.**

Craft Team Racing is a modpack for players who want to *build* the course and then *drive* it. The centrepiece is the Craft Team Racing mod: karts you craft and upgrade, tracks made of blocks and nodes, power-ups, and driving that feels like a classic kart racer.
Around it sit the tools to make tracks look and run great: building helpers, decoration, Create for contraptions and scenery, a full performance stack and shader support.

Survival is part of the fun: you start with nothing, find a Power Palm on a beach and work your way up to your first kart. Prefer to just design? Creative mode has everything.

Mods are chosen carefully, and each one should:

✅ Help you build a track, decorate it or race on it<br>
✅ Keep the vanilla feel and progression intact<br>
✅ Not add new dimensions, combat overhauls or grinding<br>
✅ Be actively maintained and play nicely with the rest of the pack<br>
✅ Look good next to everything else<br>
✅ Have little or no performance cost

Here's what to expect:

### 🏁 Craft Team Racing, ready to play
- Karts that drive through the open world: powerslides, boosts and hang time over real terrain
- A track editor made of blocks: nodes, a Track Tool, boost pads, crates and a test drive
- Power-ups, laps, positions, a podium ceremony and a Viewing Console for spectators
- A full survival progression from Power Palm to kart, with kart upgrades along the way

### 🧱 Building tools for tracks and scenery
- Building Wands and Bridging for big projects, plus Clumps and Kleeslabs for tidy builds
- Create and Copycats+ for contraptions, moving scenery and blocks that copy any look, all working with the mod's boost blocks
- Sable and Create Aeronautics included for the adventurous
- Fusion connected textures, Chipped, Handcrafted and wood-type compatibility so your grandstands and pit stops look the part
- Farm & Charm for a cozy pit-crew kitchen, and Oh The Trees You'll Grow with Better Leaves for prettier landscapes around the circuit

### ⚙ Performance-improving mods, configured and ready to go
- Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, C2ME, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and more, so karts stay smooth at 30 Hz even on busy tracks
- Sensible defaults so it runs well out of the box
- Fixes for common crashes, log spam and graphical glitches

### 👁 Client-side quality of life and visuals
- JEI and EMI for recipes, Jade for block info (it politely hides while you race), AppleSkin, Shulker Box Tooltip and inventory management
- Xaero's minimap and world map for planning your circuit, and a third-person camera mod for a better view
- Complementary shaders with Euphoria Patches ready to go through Iris, and a resource pack folder for your own look
- Freecam for scouting a track from above

### 🏘 Multiplayer-friendly
- Open Parties and Claims to protect your tracks, and Death Backup so a mistake never ruins your day
- Works for singleplayer and multiplayer; the track editor can be limited to creative players and operators

### ❗ Compatibility and balance
- Mods are tested together before they are added
- Default settings pre-configured for a good first impression
- Clear changelogs for every update

### 📋 Recommended setup
- Minecraft **1.21.1** with NeoForge **21.1.x**
- At least **6 GB** of RAM allocated; 8 GB if you plan to use shaders
