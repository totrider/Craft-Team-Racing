# <center>![Craft Team Racing title](https://i.postimg.cc/y6RDjkjB/Craft-Team-Racing-mod-icon.png)</center>

**Kart racing built into the Minecraft world: build a track from blocks, craft and upgrade your kart, and race it anywhere.**

Craft Team Racing is an attempt to bring a fun and authentic karting experience into Minecraft, adapting a classic game experience into a blocky world. You find a palm on a beach, turn its fruit into a kart, upgrade it, and then drive it across your own world, over hills, through villages and round tracks you built yourself.
A track is just blocks plus a few invisible nodes, so any build can become a circuit: a mountain pass, a Create-powered stadium, a loop round your base.

The driving is meant to feel like a classic kart racer: powerslides, hops, boosts and hang time. Its handling is ported from the open-source decompilation of *Crash Team Racing* ([ctr-native](https://github.com/CTR-tools/ctr-native)), which provided a well-tuned implementation to build on. Everything around it is specifically built for Minecraft, and new to this mod.

It works on its own: nothing else is needed. Create, Fusion, Copycats+, Jade and others only add to it.

*Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Activision, Sony, Naughty Dog or Mojang. It ships no assets from the original game.*

Here's what to expect:

### 🧱 Tracks built from blocks
- **Nodes and tools.** Place Start and Checkpoint nodes, size their collision boxes, link them with the Track Tool and test drive to validate a track
- **Branching paths**, allowing several routes across the race track
- **Boost pads, blocks, slabs and stairs**, plus a blue Super Boost family, with connected textures through Fusion and support for Create's and Copycats+ copycat blocks
- **Kart Barriers**: invisible walls that stop karts only, and only during a race
- **Crates and pickup spots** you place wherever you want, even mid-air
- **Redstone integration:** nodes and trigger crates provide redstone signals, opening up possibilities for creative redstone contraptions during races
- **Optional podium nodes** for custom built podium ceremonies with confetti
- **Viewing Console** for players who just want to spectate the racers and root for their favourites

### 🌍 Karting in the open world
- Smooth driving over real terrain: one-block steps, slabs and stairs become ramps, and no special road blocks are needed
- Surfaces come from block tags: grass and sand are slow off-road, ice is slippery, mud and soul sand drag, and a data pack can change all of it
- Karts bump and hurt mobs, boats, minecarts and players on foot
- Minecraft's damage reaches karts: an arrow spins one out, fire burns it, an explosion breaks it, and water and lava slow it down
- Pick your kart up with the Wrench and it keeps its upgrades and paint

### 🔧 Make the kart yours
- **Kart upgrades:** Offroad Tires, Soft Bumper, Floater and the lava-proof Obsidian Floater, Paddle, Snow Skis, Jump Spring and a Low Gravity Module
- **Paint** it with dyes, wash it off in a cauldron, and save **tuning profiles**
- Four engine classes as selectable tunings

### 🎁 Classic Power-ups with a Minecraft twist
- Redstone Surge, Bomb, Rocket, TNT, Potion, Slime Shield, Totem, End Crystal and Clock, each with a stronger *charged* form at 10 Power Fruit
- Place-based randomisation, so the player in last place gets the better items
- A **Rulebook**, a **Drop Table** and a **Track Music Sheet** item can configure the rules, the power-ups and the music of a race track, as a default or just the next race taking place

### 🌴 Survival progression
- Find Power Palms on beaches and in oases, harvest Power Fruit, cook Power Juice, smelt Power Ingots and craft wheels, crates and karts
- A full Power Palm wood set for building
- Every item has a recipe, and everything is in the creative tab too

### 🏁 Races for you and your friends
- A gathering area, a camera pan over the starting positions, four start lamps and a rev-for-a-boost start, laps, positions, a track bar and respawn
- A HUD that sits next to Minecraft's own interface, with lap times, places, held power-up and a speed dial

### 🔌 Mod compatibility
Craft Team Racing is a standalone mod. These optional mods add to it:
- **Fusion:** connected textures for the boost blocks
- **Create** and **Copycats+:** copycat blocks take on the block they copy, so a copycat of a boost block boosts and a copycat of ice is slippery
- **Jade:** its pop-up is hidden while you race
- **Farm & Charm:** the Bag of Power Fruit can use its sack look (optional resource pack)
- **Every Compat:** the Power Palm is picked up as a wood type, so its furniture and wood-based blocks are generated
- **Sodium** and **Iris:** tested together with shaders
- **Planned:** Sable and Create Aeronautics (karts on sub-levels and airships), Xaero's maps (the track and racers on the map) and Open Parties and Claims (claim-aware track building)
